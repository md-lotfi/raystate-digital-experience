Our products

Three apps for everyday business operations.

Each app is a separate product, designed for one kind of business. They are currently available for Android.

UBee

POS and inventory for independent shops

Run the counter from your phone: record sales, keep stock in order and know who still owes you.

For: Shop owners and small retail businesses

  • Mobile point of sale with barcode scanning
  • Product and stock management
  • Customer balances and debt settlements
  • Invoices, receipts, expenses and reports

Core operations work offline.

CoachIn

Gym memberships, sessions and attendance

Keep members, session packages and check-ins in one place, right at the front desk.

For: Gym owners, trainers, dojos and fitness studios

  • Member profiles with session packages and expiry dates
  • NFC and QR check-in
  • Attendance, payment records and digital passes
  • Cardio logs and HIIT timers

Core operations work offline.

Staffly

Staff, attendance and HR administration

Track who is in, plan schedules and handle leave and pay records from one workplace device.

For: Businesses managing a team from a shared workplace device

  • Employee profiles, attendance and breaks
  • Work schedules and leave management
  • Gross payroll calculations and recorded payments
  • Reports and document exports

Core HR workflows work offline. Employee Access is announced for the next release.

FAQ

Common questions

What does RayState build?

We make our own apps — UBee, CoachIn and Staffly — and we design and develop websites, mobile applications and custom business software for clients.

Can I use an existing RayState product instead of commissioning software?

Yes. If UBee, CoachIn or Staffly fits your business, you can start with it directly. Visit each product's website to see whether it covers your needs.

How do I start a custom project?

Email contact@raystate.com with a short description of what you want to build, who will use it and any constraints. We will discuss the requirements together before agreeing on scope.

Can the interface support English, French and Arabic?

Yes, multilingual interfaces including full right-to-left Arabic layouts are something we work with regularly. Languages are agreed as part of each project.

Are the existing products available for Android?

Yes. UBee, CoachIn and Staffly are currently available on Google Play for Android.

Do all projects work offline?

Not necessarily. Our current apps support core operations offline, but for custom projects offline support depends on the requirements and is agreed per project.

Where can I find current product pricing?

Current prices and purchase details are shown inside each app and on its product website.
